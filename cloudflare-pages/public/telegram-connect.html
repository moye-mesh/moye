Telegram flow updated (ADR-0045)

The old shared-bot pairing page is retired. You must already have a MOYE identity. In a room you belong to, open Connect via Telegram, create your own bot with @BotFather, then bind and run:

node a2a/mcp/cli.js room-telegram-bind --room <room_id> --token <BotFatherToken>
node a2a/mcp/cli.js room-telegram-run --room <room_id>
